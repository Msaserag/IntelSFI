Notes and definitions

  • Enrollment: one participant enrolled in one course-language version. A participant can hold several enrollments.
  • Participants: distinct names. Completion rate: certificates ÷ enrollments.
  • Monthly enrollments are counted by enrollment date; certificates by certificate date. The period filter uses enrollment date.
  • Avg days to certificate: certificate date minus enrollment date, certified enrollments only.
  • Country values are normalized: "Saudi", "Madina" and the Arabic spelling map to Saudi Arabia; blanks show as Unspecified.
  • Participant names are never stored in this page. They are converted to anonymous counters when a file is loaded.
